Built with Raven as copilot

Every step's "what would a cold builder ask?" question went to Raven first, logged with verdicts (raven-e6-buildlog.json): 2 helped · 3 missed. Raven routed general Soroban questions sharply (contract invocation scored 524, friendbot covered) — and missed all three confidential-stack questions at the index level: no source it holds knows OZ Confidential Tokens or the client SDK exist. Not a routing defect — a knowledge gap, and the exact one the corpus pack (D1) closes.

What this is: to our knowledge, the first agent-to-agent commerce transaction on Stellar where the price settled confidentially — two autonomous agents, a quote, an encrypted payment, decryption-verified delivery, and a signed product, end to end in ~20 seconds on testnet.
