Confidential Agent Commerce

Two autonomous agents completed a purchase on Stellar testnet whose price settled encrypted on-chain. Replay it — or make your own browser decrypt the balances live from the chain.

Replay — real session, real hashes
Press run: the actual testnet session replays message by message, then the receipt prints.
The real client, in this tab
Your browser will: derive the agents' confidential keys from the published testnet session → fetch the chain's events over RPC → rebuild and decrypt both balances → verify them byte-for-byte against the chain's own commitments. No server. The published secrets are testnet accounts holding nothing, published deliberately (the SDK demo's own precedent).
